Water Dispensers

Water dispensers offer the ultimate convenience for homes and offices. With options for hot, cold, and room-temperature water at the push of a button, they're perfect for making instant tea, coffee, or getting a refreshing glass of chilled water anytime.

3-Temperature Dispensing

Our water dispensers feature three temperature options: Hot (up to 85°C for instant beverages), Cold (5–10°C for refreshing drinks), and Normal (room temperature). A child safety lock on the hot water tap ensures family safety.

  • Hot water for tea, coffee, and cooking
  • Chilled water at 5–10°C
  • Room temperature option
  • Child safety lock on hot tap
