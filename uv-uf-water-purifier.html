UV & UF Water Purifiers

UV (Ultraviolet) and UF (Ultrafiltration) water purifiers are the ideal choice for areas with low TDS water supply. These purifiers effectively eliminate bacteria, viruses, and cysts while retaining the natural minerals in your water — giving you clean, healthy, and great-tasting drinking water.

Why Choose UV & UF Purification?

UV purification uses ultraviolet light to kill harmful microorganisms, while UF uses a hollow fiber membrane to filter out bacteria and cysts. Together, they provide double-layered protection without the need for electricity-intensive RO membranes, making them energy-efficient and eco-friendly.

  • Kills 99.9% of bacteria and viruses
  • Retains natural minerals in water
  • Low maintenance cost

Key Benefits

UV & UF purifiers are perfect for municipal or corporation water that already has a low TDS level. They don't waste water like RO systems, making them an environmentally responsible choice. Ideal for homes, offices, and small establishments.

How UV & UF Purification Works

UV Disinfection

High-intensity UV-C light destroys the DNA of bacteria, viruses, and protozoa, rendering them harmless without adding chemicals to the water.

UF Membrane Filtration

Hollow fiber UF membranes with 0.01-micron pores physically block bacteria, cysts, and suspended particles for crystal-clear water.

Mineral Retention

Unlike RO, UV+UF purification preserves essential minerals like calcium and magnesium, ensuring your water is not just safe but nutritionally balanced.
