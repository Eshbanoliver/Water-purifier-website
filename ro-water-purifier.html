RO Water Purifiers

Reverse Osmosis (RO) water purifiers are the gold standard for water purification in India. They use a semi-permeable membrane to remove dissolved impurities, heavy metals, pesticides, and harmful chemicals — delivering 100% pure and mineral-rich drinking water for your family.

Multi-Stage RO Purification

Our Kent RO purifiers use a comprehensive multi-stage purification process: Sediment Filter → Activated Carbon → RO Membrane → UV Disinfection → UF Post-filter → TDS Controller. This ensures every drop of water is free from contaminants while retaining essential minerals.

  • RO+UV+UF+TDS Control Technology
  • Removes dissolved salts and heavy metals
  • TDS controller retains essential minerals
